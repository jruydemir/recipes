Making hummus is an act of love. Thats the only way to rationalize making something so labor intensive.
It's worth it though.

Ingredients
===========

* [ ] 125g	dried chickpeas
* [ ] 1 tsp	baking soda
* [ ] 135g	light tahini paste
* [ ] 2 tbsp lemon juice
* [ ] 2	garlic cloves, crushed
* [ ] 60-100ml	ice
* [ ] 1 tsp salt

Preparation
===========
1. Soak the chickpeas overnight. I prefer to soak for a few days. If you're in a rush, put dried chickpeas into almost boiling water. Let rest for 2 hours.

2. Drain the chickpeas. Place a medium saucepan on a high heat and add the drained chickpeas and the baking soda. Cook for about 3 minutes, stirring constantly. This helps loosen the husks.

3. Add enough water to cover by an inch and bring to a boil. Skim off any foam and any husks that float to the surface. Lower heat to a slightest simmer. After 30 min check the tenderness of chickpea. Cook until tender, drain and pick off any clumps of transparent chickpea skin.

5. Place the chickpeas, garlic, lemon juice, and salt in a food processor bowl. Blend until you get a stiff paste. 

6. With the machine still running, add the tahini paste. Finally, slowly add ice and allow it to mix until you get a very smooth and creamy paste. Taste a small bit and adjust salt, lemon, and water to your preference. If making a day ahead, add a little extra water. Soft peaks should sink slightly.

7. Let the hummus rest in the fridge at least 30 minutes before eating. Ideally let it sit overnight. Serve with a drizzle of good olive oil and a pinch of sumac.
