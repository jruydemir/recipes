This isn't perfect, but it is a GOOD Babaganoug recipe.

Ingredients
===========

* [ ] 1 eggplant or 650g of jarred eggplant
* [ ] 2 tbsp lemon juice
* [ ] 2 cloves garlic
* [ ] 3 tbsp tahini
* [ ] 1/4 tsp salt

Preparation
===========
1. Poke holes in an eggplant using a fork. Wrap the eggplant in foil, and then grill or roast at 400F for 1 hour. Turn every 15 minutes. Remove and allow to cool.

2. Coursely chop the eggplant and allow to drain in a mesh sieve for 15 minutes. Remove a 1/4 of the eggplant and finely mince into a paste. 

3. In a medium bowl, grate garlic, add lemon juice, salt, and tahini. Additionally add the course and finely minced eggplant. Mix well.

4. If you used jarred eggplant, or roasted your eggplant, then do the following to add a smoked element. Make a small well in the babaganoug. Place a 2" x 2" piece of foil and form into the well. Add a tablespoon of olive oil to the foil. Now heat a small piece of charcoal over a burner until red (VENT on high). Place the coal in the oil. Cover for 5 minutes. Afterwards toss the oil, charcoal and foil. Mix the babaganoug again.  

5. Put into a two deli containers, and let rest in the fridge for at least an hour. Serve with fresh bread and olive oil. It'll stay good in the fridge for a week, you can freeze it for up to 3 months.
