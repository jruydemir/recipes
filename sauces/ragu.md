* [ ] 2 pounds ground beef, pork or lamb
* [ ] 2 med carrot, grated
* [ ] 3 celery ribs, minced
* [ ] 1 medium yellow onion finely chopped, about ¾ cup
* [ ] 5 garlic cloves, minced
* [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
* [ ] 1 15oz can diced tomatoes
* [ ] 1 15oz can tomato sauce
* [ ] 3/4 tsp salt
* [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
* [ ] 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
* [ ] 1 tsp granulated sugar
* [ ] 1 cup chicken, or beef stock
* [ ] Parm 

Preparation
===========
1. In a hot pan over medium high heat saute beef until well browned, and beginning to stick to the pot. Add carrot, celery, and onion for 5 minutes until tender. Add garlic, and tomato paste.
2. Push ingredients to a corner of the pan and brown beef. Mix and saute for another 10 minutes.
3. Add remaining ingredients. Lower to a simmer and cook until reduced by about half. ~60-90 minutes. Should decently stick to the back of a spoon.
4. Add fresh grated parm to taste. Sauce should be slightly salty.

Noteas
=====
Can add half a cup of lentils with an extra cup of broth.
